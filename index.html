01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

G23 Evidence Copilot

A deterministic evidence-verification and claim-control prototype.

G23 is designed to check defined evidence properties and keep verifier-owned results separate from AI-generated explanations. Its purpose is to make the basis of a claim easier to inspect and audit.

02 / VERIFICATION STATES

Results with explicit boundaries

G23 uses four states to communicate the outcome of its defined checks.

VALID

Checks passed

The defined verification checks passed. This is not a blanket guarantee of runtime correctness or authenticity.

UNKNOWN

Not established

The available evidence is insufficient to establish the claim.

MISMATCH

Does not match

The observed evidence conflicts with an expected value or defined condition.

CORRUPTED

Invalid or damaged

The evidence cannot be accepted because its structure or integrity check failed.

03 / EVIDENCE INTEGRITY

Make evidence inspectable

01

Deterministic checks

Use explicit rules to evaluate evidence rather than treating generated explanations as verification.

02

Cryptographic references

SHA-256 hashes and hash-chain checks can help detect changes to recorded evidence.

03

AI boundary

AI-generated explanations are non-authoritative and must not override verifier-owned states.

Scope and limitations

G23 is a general-purpose software verification and evidence-integrity prototype. A VALID result means only that the checks implemented for that result passed. It does not independently prove runtime correctness, production readiness, source authenticity, or the truth of every underlying claim. G23 is not a medical AI system or clinical decision-support tool.
